# initial-conditions-perturbed-fields: authoring notes

This directory is hidden at Harbor runtime and is not part of the contract.
`comment/pipeline/` is written only by the CLI (module entry, test survey,
self-validation and runtime records). This file is the human-readable story.

## Module

The module combines InitialConditions.c/.h with PerturbedField.c/.h because perturbation directly consumes the seeded density and 1LPT/2LPT displacement grids. It covers Gaussian initial modes, density/displacement generation, relative velocities, and linear, Zel'dovich or 2LPT matter evolution. Halo/source deposition, ionisation, thermal/X-ray evolution, brightness temperature and coeval/lightcone orchestration are deliberately excluded because the owner approved only this upstream matter-field module.

## Tolerances

The bounds begin from official physical anchors, upstream regression observables, analytic identities and measured numerical floors. Most variants move one active input by two float32 ulps. The two synthetic translation checks instead move the selected displacement by 128 ulps because a sensitivity sweep found that cloud-in-cell arithmetic rounded away probes through 64 ulps and first changed output at 96. The first Docker calibration's injected-density result is rejected as tolerance evidence: its single-cell field contained delta=342999, carried broad Fourier support and elicited a non-local quadratic 2LPT response outside the useful perturbative regime; its nominal float64 mean compensation was also rounded away at the float32 precision consumed by the code. The human owner instead selected a smooth, low-amplitude, multi-directional float32 field with exact pairwise zero mean and approved its uniform pointwise atol=2e-5, rtol=0 after a one/two/four-ulp sweep and wrong-normalisation, wrong-direction, omitted-2LPT and shifted-grid probes. The formal two-ulp variant's largest spread was 2.384185791015625e-7, leaving at least 83.9-fold headroom.

All random-field checks were redesigned after one-thread experiments showed that 21cmFAST changes the concrete realization even at fixed seed: low-k band power moved by roughly 11--29%. The three low-resolution evolution checks were then tested on five additional seeds. All six realization probes pass their broad-band envelopes; the closest bound fractions are 0.947 (default 2LPT), 0.972 (LINEAR) and 0.963 (Zel'dovich). Only LINEAR mid-k velocity required adjustment, from 5% to the observed-and-rounded 8% envelope after a 6.11% maximum shift. Strict within-realization physical relations and conservation laws are calibrated separately. The low-resolution default check pairs 2LPT with same-seed Zel'dovich and measures 5.58% density and 2.36% velocity corrections; the Zel'dovich check pairs with LINEAR and measures 77.4%/17.1%. The LINEAR check verifies the shared delta>-1 clipping after D(z) scaling, initial--final coherence/transfer, the i*k_z/k^2 velocity amplitude and structure, phase, zero modes and longitudinal dominance. The high-resolution check measures a 7.09%/2.61% 2LPT correction. Existing omitted-2LPT, wrong-branch, resolution-substitution, density/velocity-misalignment, CLASS-axis, relative-velocity-unit and transfer probes fail decisively. This calibration remains limited to one arm64 host and does not establish a portable alternative-build floor.

## Blind spots

Native evidence currently comes from one arm64 macOS host; the smooth injected-density pointwise check is the highest-priority x86/A100 reproduction target before scientific acceptance. Upstream skips its synthetic high-resolution 2LPT roll test because downsampling aliases the expected translation, so the high-resolution branch uses scale spectra, one-point summaries, conservation and within-field relations rather than a full-grid reference. Random phases are deliberately ungraded: globally translating scalar `lowres_vcb` passes exactly, and production does not expose its vector components. The Muñoz21 leaf is demonstrably equivalent to ordinary CLASS plus relative velocities at this module boundary, so it claims official tutorial-configuration coverage only. The task does not claim downstream halo, source, ionisation, thermal or brightness-temperature physics.
