Sepal diffusion scoring and neighborhood-enrichment permutation counting		approvedThis is one module because both entrypoints have exactly the same contract - (CSR spatial connectivity graph in adata.obsp, a per-observation signal) in, a score matrix out - both are implemented as owned numba kernels that sweep that signal over the graph many times, and both are numerically self-contained: they read the graph, they never rebuild it, and they touch nothing in im, pl, tl, experimental, _ligrec or _niche.
