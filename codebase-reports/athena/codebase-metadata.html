### Corrections and conservative decisions

- The chemistry task abbreviates the preprint title and omits/reorders authors. Gong2023Chemistry uses the exact title and complete nine-author list verified at arXiv:2305.04965; the upstream Chemistry wiki explicitly requests this work.
- The Newtonian-MHD task combines two shearing-box works under DOI 10.1086/533428. That DOI identifies Matt and Pudritz on stellar winds, not Johnson et al. Use separate Johnson2008OrbitalAdvection (10.1086/586707, independently linked by arXiv:0804.4699) and Johnson2005ShearingWaves (10.1086/430081, arXiv:astro-ph/0501005). The pinned jgg.cpp and ssheet.cpp confirm the intended works.
- The radiation task gives DOI 10.3847/1538-4365/ac9b40, which returned 404. The upstream radiation wiki identifies Jiang (2022), ApJS 263, 4; Jiang2022Multigroup conservatively cites the matching verified arXiv:2209.06240 instead. No journal DOI is invented and no duplicate journal/preprint entry is added.
- The self-gravity task calls the Plimpton paper 1997, but its DOI records publication in 1995. Plimpton1995ParallelAlgorithms is retained only as historical parallel-software context, not evidence that an MD workload is shipped or that the article describes Athena FFT routines. Frigo2005FFTW directly covers the FFTW library used in src/fft/athena_fft.cpp.
- The SR and GR task references combine the Mignone-Bodo (2005) and Mignone-Ugliano-Bodo (2009) papers under a repository URL. They are separated into two verified DOI entries and shared by both task mappings.
- The pinned README cites software release v24.0, whereas its CITATION.cff still describes v21.0. Athena2024Software follows the README and verified Zenodo record; neither release is asserted to be identical to the benchmark source commit.
- Capitalization, TeX-safe punctuation, and obvious Ryu (1995) DOI-title typographical errors are normalized. The combined III/IV Hawley-Balbus title is retained as registered. Published papers and their arXiv versions occupy a single entry where both identifiers were verified.

## Gaps and warnings

- This is an informational bibliography-led backfill from shipped task evidence, not a regenerated source audit or a new module approval. Source fingerprint, file/line accounting and framework-collected test counts remain unknown (null).
- Seventy shipped check directories are counted statically; they are not seventy newly executed tests, individual parameter cases, or total upstream tests. No native, Docker, GPU, calibration, runtime or speedup validation was performed for this documentation-only change.
- All eight shipped task manifests declare draft status. Existing pass policies and approval records were read, not re-approved or modified.
- The GR runtime checks use Minkowski coordinates; curved-metric coverage is not established by these checks.
- Cosmic-ray transport is not one of the eight shipped tasks; chemistry six-ray radiation is distinct from the radiation-transport task.
- BSD-3-Clause is the upstream/task declaration, not a completed file-level license audit. The pinned src/nr_radiation/integrators/multi_group.cpp also contains a GPL header; this backfill does not resolve that discrepancy.
- The chemistry and multigroup works are recorded as verified arXiv preprints; unverified journal metadata are omitted.
